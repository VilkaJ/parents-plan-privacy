PARENTS PLAN

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026  |  Application ID: si.parentsplan.app  |  Provider: Julian Zupan Arsov s.p. (Slovenia, EU)

Julian Zupan Arsov s.p. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your family's privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Parents Plan mobile application handles user information, device permissions, third-party advertising, and family records.

Privacy-First Design: Parents Plan stores all your family data, child profiles, sleep logs, meal plans, and photos locally on your device. We do not operate external cloud databases that collect or store your personal family records.

1. Intended Audience (Adults 18+ Only)

Parents Plan is designed strictly for use by adults aged 18 and older (parents and legal guardians). Children are not permitted to create accounts or use the application directly. Any child-related details (such as first names, birth dates, and profile photos) are entered and controlled exclusively by the parent or legal guardian.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core organizational features, the app stores the following information locally within your device's private application storage:

  • Account & Preferences: Your email address, parent role (Mum/Dad), theme preference (Light/Dark mode), and timestamp of your Terms & Privacy consent.
  • Child Profiles: Child's name, birth date (used locally to calculate age in months and tailor meal suggestions), and sleep/awake timestamps.
  • Daily Organization: To-do lists, weekly meal plans, grocery shopping lists, and calendar events/birthdays.
  • Profile Photos: Optional photos selected from your gallery or captured with your camera for parent and child avatars.

3. Device Permissions We Request

Parents Plan requests system permissions only when you actively use specific features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely if you choose to take a new profile photo for yourself or your child. Photos are saved locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Photo Gallery / Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used solely when you choose an existing image from your device gallery for a profile avatar.
  • Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to deliver local reminders 3 days and 1 day prior to your scheduled calendar events and birthdays.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Parents Plan free for parents, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google AdMob may collect and process certain device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, general location (country/city level), and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads.

You can manage your ad personalization preferences or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads. For more information on how Google uses data, please review Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

5. PDF Exports

When you export your Weekly Menu to a PDF file, the document is generated locally on your device and saved only to the location you select via the Android system file picker.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have full control over your personal data:

  • Access & Modification: You can view, edit, or delete individual calendar events, shopping items, tasks, and meal entries directly inside the app at any time.
  • Complete Erasure: Because all family data resides locally on your device, you can permanently erase all application data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if new features or advertising partners are added to Parents Plan. Any material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page and inside the app.
